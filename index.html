Less platform.
More protocol.

nullnet removes the central service from the conversation path. What remains is a focused native client, an open protocol, and the shortest viable route between two peers.

PRIVATE CONNECTIONS, BY DESIGN

Your keys.
Your conversations.

Cryptographic identities replace centralized accounts. TLS 1.3 protects peer traffic end to end, including when a relay helps carry packets.

⌘

Your identity belongs to you.

Ed25519 keys identify peers without an email address or central account. Share your public identity; keep the private key secret.

✓

Only peers can read conversation traffic.

TLS 1.3 protects contents between connected peer clients. Relays carry ciphertext, not message plaintext.

↗

Network details may still be visible to relays.

A TURN operator may observe IP addresses, timing, and traffic volume.

⌂

Protect the device that holds your keys.

Local SQLite history and identity material are not encrypted at rest today.

RELAY & ROOMS

Discovery is not
the conversation.

A rendezvous service helps peers discover each other. A relay can carry traffic when a direct route is unavailable.

SERVER ADDRESSUse the relay address configured for your build.

No public relay endpoint is published in the current project information.

AVAILABILITYDepends on the configured operator.

There is no uptime commitment stated here. Direct peer connections do not depend on an always-on message inbox.

ROOM USAGEUse rooms supported by your client.

Room discovery and membership are client/protocol features; a relay transports packets and should not be treated as message storage.

WHEN SOMETHING GOES WRONG

Quick checks.
Clear next steps.

These checks address common build, certificate, and connectivity failures. Include the exact error and your platform when asking for help.

Build fails or a dependency is missing +

Follow the build instructions in the repository for your operating system, install the listed compiler and dependencies, then start from a clean build directory. Include the first compiler error in an issue if it persists.

Certificate or TLS verification fails +

Check that the system clock is correct and the certificate matches the peer or relay you intended to reach. Do not disable certificate verification to work around the error; report the certificate message and connection target.

Peers cannot connect directly +

Confirm both clients are online and using compatible builds. Some NATs and firewalls block direct paths; configure a supported relay and retry. Share the ICE/connection status in a support issue, but remove private keys and sensitive logs.
